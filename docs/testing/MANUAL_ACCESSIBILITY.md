# Manual accessibility checklist

Automated axe checks cover the public portal on desktop and mobile viewports. Before pilot, an assigned tester must record browser, assistive technology and result for:

- keyboard-only navigation, visible focus and skip link on applicant and staff routes;
- form label/error announcement, required state and conflict alerts;
- 200% zoom and 320 CSS-pixel layout without horizontal loss of form actions;
- screen-reader reading order for application sections, status timeline and evidence workbench;
- evidence source highlight being conveyed by text/page/field context, not colour alone;
- offline, saving, failed and synced states announced and understandable;
- PDF acknowledgement and invitation document-tag/accessibility review.

Critical or serious barriers block pilot sign-off. Record exceptions with owner, workaround and remediation date.
