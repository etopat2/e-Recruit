# Training/PATS guide

Issue a training invitation only for an approved final selection. Confirm reporting date/time/location/instructions and the generated private document before issue. Record Reported, Late, No Show, Declined or the approved status with minimal notes and the actual time.

Reserve replacement requires an available entry from the same certified run/bucket, explicit reason and approval reference. The service marks the replaced selection, consumes the reserve entry and creates the replacement selection atomically; never choose a reserve informally. Reconcile invitations/reporting/replacements with audit records and notify through approved channels.
