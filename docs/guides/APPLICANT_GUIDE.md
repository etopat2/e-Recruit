# Applicant guide

Use the official UPS e-Recruit address shown in the authorised campaign notice. Create one account with your own contact and identification details; never share your password or verification code. Select an open post, complete each configured section, upload clear PDF/JPEG/PNG evidence and watch the save indicator. Offline drafts remain on that device until reconnected; do not submit until it shows “Saved securely.”

Review the declaration and submit once. Successful submission locks the snapshot and issues a UPS reference, QR and acknowledgement. Keep the reference; it does not reveal or guarantee an interview centre. If hard copies are required, follow the campaign deadline and instructions for final receipt at Uganda Prisons Service Headquarters. A unit or region may transmit the packet, but only an authorised verification officer records the accountable receipt; interview centres and panels do not receive it.

Track status, notices and any appeal in the portal. UPS decisions are based on authorised human verification; OCR assists document reading only. Contact the in-portal helpdesk for a problem and do not send NIN/document images through unofficial channels.
