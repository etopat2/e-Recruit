# Medical user guide

Medical officers access only assigned candidates/schedules and record one controlled outcome: Fit, Not Fit, Deferred, Further Assessment Required or No Show. Clinical reference/restricted notes are visible only through the medical policy. Put only necessary information in those fields; do not include clinical detail in general helpdesk/audit reasons.

A changed record uses its entity version and returns a visible conflict instead of overwriting. Council final approval requires a certified selected outcome and the same candidate’s Fit result. Council and recruitment users receive the gate result, not restricted clinical notes. Correct errors through the audited/versioned process and escalate privacy incidents immediately.
