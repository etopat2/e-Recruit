# Verification officer guide

Open only assigned/scoped applications. The workbench keeps the applicant declaration, original protected evidence, extracted fields/confidence/source location, comparisons and decision controls together. Select a field to locate its page/bounding source where available. OCR and similarity are suggestions, not authority.

Compare sources pairwise. Record a versioned verified value only from the cited source, or record a discrepancy/needs-review decision with an appropriate reason. Never use majority vote across documents, edit the original, expose a public link or reject solely because OCR is unavailable/low-confidence. Hard-copy receipt is separate evidence and does not silently replace the online source.

Escalate suspected tampering/malware/identity mismatch through the authorised workflow and keep PII out of tickets/logs.
