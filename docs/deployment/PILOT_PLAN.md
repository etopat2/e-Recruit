# Controlled pilot plan

UPS selects the pilot centre(s); this plan deliberately does not invent them.

## Entry conditions

- Approved infrastructure, TLS, synthetic-only rehearsal tenant/data, current backup and restore evidence.
- Authoritative geography/centre mapping and campaign rules signed by Recruitment.
- Named pilot lead, centre coordinator, panel head, verification, medical, training, IT/security, helpdesk and incident contacts.
- Managed devices inventoried, patched, encrypted, screen-lock enforced and time synchronised; browsers/PWA installed.
- Staff accounts least-privileged, scoped and MFA-enrolled; recovery process rehearsed.

## Rehearsal

1. Create a synthetic campaign and candidates; validate reference/QR/acknowledgement and hard-copy receipt.
2. Download one scoped pack per registered device, disconnect the network, refresh/reopen the PWA and verify the permitted roster remains available.
3. Record QR/manual attendance and scores offline; check pending counters and prohibition of out-of-scope actions.
4. Produce a protected-field two-device conflict, reconnect, sync and have an authorised officer resolve it with justification.
5. Close the panel only after required scores/sync; run ranking/selection scenario and confirm fingerprints/quotas.
6. Record synthetic medical outcomes, Council approval, invitation, non-reporting and reserve replacement.
7. Exercise a worker outage, notification failure, device revocation and helpdesk escalation.
8. Reconcile server/package event counts, failed jobs, open conflicts and device custody at day end.

## Pilot day controls

Take a verified backup before opening. Download packs only inside the approved window. Never share accounts/devices. Keep a paper fallback identifier log approved by UPS without copying unnecessary PII. Sync at scheduled checkpoints and before pack expiry. The pilot lead records incidents and correlation IDs; security leads containment for loss/exposure; operations executes rollback/recovery.

## Exit/sign-off

Reconcile all packages/events/conflicts, inspect audit chain and role/scope exceptions, review accessibility/helpdesk feedback, compare observed latency/availability to targets, inventory every device, and complete Recruitment, Operations, Security/Privacy and pilot-centre sign-off. Production expansion remains blocked until critical defects are closed or formally risk-accepted by authorised owners.
